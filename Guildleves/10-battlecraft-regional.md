# 10 — Battlecraft (Regional combat) guildleves

For Disciples of War and Magic. Always pays **gil or Guild Marks + treasure**;
**30-minute** timer; difficulty **1–5 stars**; optional Guardian bonus;
Victory Fanfare on completion. New sets as any class hits **5 / 13 / 23 / 33**
(level 1 at start). All plate descriptions VERIFIED via [Fandom 1.0].

Core loop shared by every type: start at the camp aetheryte/aetherial gate →
"slay all monsters before time runs out," with the plate-specific gimmick
below. Treasure chests may spawn en route (better odds in bigger parties).

## The 8 plates (each = one objective ruleset)

### 1. Diligence — loot-hunt kills
- **Plate art:** Saint Tothor the Ratcatcher, a Lalafell holding two slain
  moles.
- **Objective:** kill monsters to acquire a quota of a **specific, randomly
  dropped loot item**.
- **Respawn rule:** if you kill everything before the quota is filled, **new
  monsters spawn** until time expires or the quota completes. Never a dead
  leve — keep killing.

### 2. Valor — straight extermination
- **Plate art:** Saint Daniffen and the Basilisk — a Lancer impaling the
  monster.
- **Objective:** kill **all** leve monsters. No gimmick; the baseline and the
  tutorial-leve type (below).

### 3. Tenacity — chase the runner
- **Plate art:** an Elezen archer riding a chocobo through a forest.
- **Objective:** a monster **flees — sometimes very far — and must be followed**
  to a spot where **reinforcements spawn and attack**.
- **Trap:** killing the runner early does NOT skip the reinforcements — they
  must still be killed.

### 4. Veracity — find the hidden true target
- **Plate art:** a female Lalafell posing beside a statue.
- **Objective:** the real target is **hidden**. Reveal it either with a
  **special item dropped by a specific co-present monster**, or by
  **attacking suspects** — striking the true one makes it disappear and
  transform into the real monster.

### 5. Wisdom — "Necrologos" page hunt (2 variants)
- **Plate art:** a bard playing while his ally fights.
- **Variant A (drop hunt):** kill monsters that drop **Necrologos Pages**;
  collecting them all spawns a **final monster** — kill it to finish.
- **Variant B (light pillars):** small **pillars of light** appear in an area;
  inspect each: nothing, an "unexpected monster," or a Necrologos Page. All
  pages → final monster set → kill them.
- Name note: "Necrologos" titles also exist in ARR; only the *mechanic* above
  is VERIFIED 1.0. Individual `Necrologos: …` titles in the catalog are
  NEEDS-VERIFY for era.

### 6. Justice — keyed light-pillar search
- **Plate art:** a Roegadyn knight and an Elezen elder.
- **Objective:** find target monsters by interacting with **light pillars** —
  but interaction requires a **specific item first obtained by killing other
  creatures** around the lights. Kill → key item → pillars → targets.

### 7. Temperance — patrol route
- **Plate art:** a Miqo'te carrying a vase by a body of water.
- **Objective:** **patrol**: walk to designated lights in sequence; finishing
  the patrol **spawns the target monsters** — kill them.

### 8. Devotion — timed defense point
- **Plate art:** a shirtless Hyur warrior with sword and large shield.
- **Objective:** **defend a fixed spot for several minutes**; when the defense
  timer hits 0, one **final monster set** spawns — defeat it.

## Tutorial Battlecraft leves
- Easier intro leves for beginners: always **Valor type, level 1**,
  significantly smaller rewards. [Fandom 1.0]

## Grand Company Battlecraft leves (solo-only, issued in the field)
- Available from **Private Third Class**; issued not by the Adventurers' Guild
  but by **company officers at field locations**; can be **taken and started
  immediately on location**. Cost allowances like normal. Rewards primarily
  **company seals**. [Fandom 1.0]
- **Promptitude** — plate: a warrior mounting an antelope-like beast.
  Objective: kill **as many enemies as possible in 15 minutes** (score-attack,
  not extermination).
- **Equity** — plate: a judge holding a balanced scale; stained glass split
  angel-wing / devil-wing. Objective: use the **/poke emote** on creatures to
  find which holds a **special item**, kill those, then use the item on the
  others to reveal the **true levequest targets**.
