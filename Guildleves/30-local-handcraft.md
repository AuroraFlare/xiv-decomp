# 30 — Local (Handcraft) guildleves

For Disciples of the Hand (all 8 crafting classes). Always pays **gil or
Guild Marks**. New sets **every 5 levels** from level 1. No field timer —
craft at your leisure, then deliver. All VERIFIED via [Fandom 1.0] and
[GameSpot primer].

## The 2 plates

### 1. Constancy — materials up front
- **Plate art:** Saint Moergynn and the Edgeless Blade.
- **Flow:** materials are **handed to you at accept** — craft immediately.
- Delivery NPC is the standard turn-in; no pre-craft errand.

### 2. Ingenuity — fetch materials from the client first
- **Plate art:** a Hyur woman giving bread to another Hyur.
- **Flow:** you must **talk to the levequest NPC first to receive the
  materials**, then craft. (The defining difference from Constancy.)
- Client NPCs sit in town or at camps, **almost always in safe locations**.
  [GameSpot primer]

## The crafting loop (both types)
1. Accept at the Local counter (Constancy: +materials; Ingenuity: go see the
   client for materials).
2. Open crafting, choose **"Quest Synthesis,"** pick the levequest.
3. Synthesize normally until the **required quota** is made — the quest
   completes on the quota item.
4. **Keep going:** leftover materials should be burned through anyway — it's
   effectively **free EXP**. [Fandom 1.0]
5. Deliver to the client NPC.

## Item rules (launch-era, GameSpot)
- Leve items are **free** (no cost to you), take **no inventory space**, and
  **cannot be equipped or sold** to anyone but the stated NPC.

## Guild marks (crafters)
- No class-change penalty (unlike battle/field). Higher **performance rating**
  → more likely to draw marks instead of gil. Marks from level 20+ city leves
  (file 00 §11). [Fandom 1.0]
