# 40 — Faction leves (high-risk, high-reward)

Special levequests that can be **either battle or fieldcraft** in nature.
Familiar to any player **over level 10**. [Engadget/Massively, Dec 2010]

## The three factions (VERIFIED, GameSpot)
- **The Horn and Hand**
- **The Brotherhood of the Broken Blade**
- **Azeyma's Shields**

## Earning and spending standing
- Completing regional (battle/field) leves earns **favor/standing** with a
  faction. [GameSpot, Engadget]
- Launch-era rule: at **100 favor** with a faction you may undertake one of
  its faction leves — which **depletes the stock**, so you must earn 100
  again for the next. [GameSpot]
- Late-1.0 wording: spend **Faction Points** earned from completed guildleves
  to get faction leves. [Fandom 1.0] (Same system, renamed currency.)
- Successful completion can unlock **more advanced follow-up leves**
  (faction leve tiers/chains). [Engadget]

## How a faction leve runs
- **40-minute timer** (not 30). [Fandom 1.0]
- You must speak with a **specific NPC after beginning AND after finishing**
  the leve (bookend NPCs). [Fandom 1.0]
- Battle-faction leves run the **Valor or Tenacity** rulesets but with
  **stronger monsters, including foes seen nowhere else**. [Fandom 1.0]
- Otherwise identical to normal battle/field leves (difficulty, coffers,
  fanfare). Victory Fanfare plays. [Fandom 1.0]

## Rewards
- **Higher gil than a normal leve of the same level**, plus **Unique equipment**
  — most of it upgradeable into very strong gear. [Fandom 1.0]
- Faction leves in history boost **leve evaluation** payouts (file 00 §10);
  the max known 90k bonus needs 5 Coerthas + 3 other-city faction leves.

## Faction-only plates (Horn and Hand)
- **Hability** (Horn and Hand only) — plate: a shepherd leading his flock
  with a villainous figure behind a tree. Objective: **stalk/sneak after a
  creature to a destination without being detected**; spotted = target resets
  to the start. [Fandom 1.0]
- **Industry** (Horn and Hand only) — plate: a man crafting a golden plate.
  Objective: **search an area for scattered items**. [Fandom 1.0]
