# FFXIV 1.0 Guildleves — Research Pack

Date: 2026-09-27. Scope: **version 1.0 only** (launch 2010 through 1.23b,
pre–A Realm Reborn). ARR reuses many names with different rules; ARR rules are
out of scope except where noted as contrast.

This folder documents the 1.0 guildleve system three ways:

1. **How the system works** (allowances, plates, difficulty, party rules).
2. **How each guildleve type works** (every Regional/Battlecraft,
   Fieldcraft, Local/Handcraft, Faction, and Grand Company plate, with its
   objective loop).
3. **Individual levequests** (verified examples + a starter catalog; the
   exhaustive per-leve list of several hundred 1.0 leves is OPEN — see below).

## Legend (all files)

- **VERIFIED**: confirmed by a named source in SOURCES.md (wiki text, press
  guide, video title/content, Lodestone achievement text).
- **NEEDS-VERIFY**: plausible 1.0 leve/name but era or details unconfirmed —
  confirm against 1.0 client data or period footage before citing as fact.
- **OPEN**: information not recoverable from public sources; needs 1.0
  game-data mining (DATs/LUA) or period captures.

## Honest scope note

A literally exhaustive list of **every individual 1.0 levequest** (several
hundred across 3 city-states, 5 rank bands, 3 disciplines, plus faction and
company leves) does not survive in any single public source: the Gamer Escape
`Removed Levequest` category mixes 1.0 removals with later removals, and the
old 1.0 databases (gamercorner tables) are partly offline. So this pack gives
you **complete per-type coverage** (every plate's objective loop — the part
that defines "how each guildleve works") plus **verified individual examples**
per type, and points at the lookup indexes for deep per-leve research. The
catalog CSV is designed to grow as you mine names from client data.
