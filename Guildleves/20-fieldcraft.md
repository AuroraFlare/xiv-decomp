# 20 — Fieldcraft (Regional gathering) guildleves

For Disciples of the Land (Miner, Botanist, Fisher). Always pays **gil or
Guild Marks + treasure**; **30-minute** timer; optional Guardian bonus;
Victory Fanfare on completion. Level-1 leves at start, new sets when the
matching DoL class hits **15 / 25 / 35**. Start at the specified **camp
aetheryte**. Treasure chests may spawn in the field (gil always, sometimes
items/gear to the player's loot list). All VERIFIED via [Fandom 1.0].

## The 2 plates

### 1. Munificence — quota of a leve-only item
- **Plate art:** a male Lalafell carrying many goods.
- **Objective:** gather a quota (usually **3**) of a **specific item within
  the timer**.
- **Node rule:** the item appears **randomly per attempt, only on the leve's
  own gathering nodes** — never on normal map nodes.
- **EXP rule:** each leve-item gather pays **~3x normal EXP**; the Guardian
  bonus applies **only to the levequest item**, not to everything gathered.

### 2. Piety — exhaust three nodes
- **Plate art:** a Miqo'te fishing.
- **Objective:** gather on **three different nodes until each is exhausted**.
  No quota item — completion is by depletion.
- **EXP rule:** the Guardian bonus applies to **every successful gather**
  (unlike Munificence).

## Tutorial Fieldcraft leves
- Easier intro leves: always **Piety type, level 1**, significantly smaller
  rewards. [Fandom 1.0]
