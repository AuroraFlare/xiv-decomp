# Level-30 regional guildleves — 2026-09-07

The 24 outdoor battlecraft leves at Cedarwood, Nophica's Wells and Humblehearth
now have encounter files. Their party composition, kill objectives, collection
steps, fleeing survivors, disguise searches and Necrologos finales use the
checked-in eLeMeN archive and the current SQL objective slots. The shared
`_level30.lua` builder also supplies the 24 underground encounters authored in
`_level30_dungeons.lua`; the behavior validator executes all 48.

These are functional reconstructions with explicit placement and tuning
provenance. No level-30 `!glbuild` encounter captures were found in
`C:/serverdata/guildleve_authoring`. The outdoor locations use connected recorded
nodes from `zone_128.tsv`, `zone_172.tsv` and `zone_150.tsv`, generated by
`tools/build_regional_guildleve_placements.py`. They preserve recorded ground
heights and use graph edges for escape routes. They are **not retail encounter
coordinates**. The underground pack uses offsets around existing `!mypos`
captures where its zones lack a navigation graph; those offsets require a
terrain pass. Both statuses remain visible in the returned encounter config.

## Evidence and unresolved details

- **Escape Artist:** the 2010-11-01 recording shows two Fire Elementals at 0:54
  and a Darkwing Devilet objective of 0/2 at 2:36. This supports the Japanese
  archive and current DAT elemental slot. The English commission instead says
  Ocean Roseling; the discrepancy is preserved in `evidenceStatus`, rather
  than silently claiming all versions used the same disguise.
- **Secret of the Sewers:** the archive omits its objective flow. The
  2011-01-22 recording at 2:31 shows Darkwing Devilet 0/2, Fire Elemental 0/2,
  and a defeated Longclaw Galago in the combat log. The implemented reveal
  agrees with those frames, the commission and current DAT slots.
- **Elemental Thralldom:** full-size footage at 1:35 identifies Fire Bomb and
  both page counters at 1/3; later frames show earth and wind elementals. A
  nearby Fat Dodo in a contact sheet was ambient, and was not used as an
  encounter target. The implementation retains the archived bomb ambush.
- **Lords of Skyey Realms:** the 2:04 combat log contains two Downcast Hippocerf
  defeats and the final group defeat before completion. The summoned actors
  use that named guildleve variant (`2200401`).
- The archive marks the old disguise overviews for 10845 and 12485 as not
  reconfirmed after Patch 1.19. Current target counts and actor slots are
  preserved, but candidate population and placement still require capture.
- Item/page drop chance **75%**, search success chance **65%**, three replacement
  areas, three disguise candidates and the chase **50% HP** threshold are
  provisional tuning where direct evidence is absent. These are stated in
  `tuningStatus`; they are not measured retail probabilities. Correct disguise
  counts are guaranteed within a randomized candidate set, so a run cannot
  become unwinnable because all generated candidates were false.
- Search failures spawn their archived ambush, award no page, consume the
  point, and reopen that point when its ambush dies. Exact retail point
  relocation/cadence remains uncaptured. Collection and final combat remain
  distinct requirements.

## Completion, links and map behavior

Necrologos configs use `manualCompletion=true`. Summoned enemies receive a
separate `summoned` role and no page-counter credit. Completion requires every
page counter and every summoned wave to be clear. Restricting the terminal
trigger to summoned deaths is essential: the engine processes `mob_kill`
before `objective_progress`, so the last page-bearing source must not complete
the leve before its summoning trigger executes. Finales appear immediately
when their pages are collected, even if other source enemies remain.

Party IDs are allocated across the entire encounter. Simultaneous traps,
separate search candidates, donor enemies and summoned books cannot merge
into one linked party accidentally. Multi-circle search and disguise configs
retain all three initial markers when a local ambush or reveal occurs.

The optional resource-enabled disguise builder supports the underground
Bomb Eye/Brightash variants. Reagent donors award no target credit. Both the
content command and the first damaging hit require payment; wrong candidates
resolve in place and correct candidates replace their entire protected pair.

## Validation

Run `python tools/validate_guildleves_level30.py` from the repository. It loads
the actual numeric files, builders, placements and `GuildleveFirstSet.lua`
into MoonSharp, then executes them with a director that models actor rosters,
death, objective completion, action batches, resources and coroutine timing.

The 48-encounter run passed. Its checks cover delayed reinforcement boundaries,
failed item drops and replenishment, last-page immediate finales, no completion
from pages alone, failed ground searches and retries, both books required
before completion, wrong disguise kills, protected pair transformations,
empty reagent stock, paid command/hit reveals, single and double chase routes,
the zero-kill flee threshold, nonzero SQL mob slots, distinct linked parties,
and preservation of simultaneous map circles. It does not substitute for an
in-game terrain/combat pass.
