### Grand Company quests (titles unidentified)

Patch-note reference: `[dev1003]`

Grand Company quests were implemented for all three city-states, but the patch notes do not list their titles. The first quest for each company requires rank 22 in any Discipline of War or Magic.

| Grand Company | Headquarters | Quest contact | Seal currency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Maelstrom | Maelstrom Command (Limsa Lominsa) | Storm Sergeant Second Class Grizzly Gnat | Storm Seals |
| The Order of the Twin Adder | The Adders' Nest (Gridania) | Serpent Sergeant Second Class Sorainne Haurtelle | Serpent Seals |
| The Immortal Flames | The Hall of Flames (Ul'dah) | Flame Sergeant Second Class U'sanzi Rahz | Flame Seals |

Grand Company headquarters use the same entrances as the market areas. Quest completion awards company-specific seals based on task difficulty; these seals can be exchanged for items.

#### Official enlistment quests

These three quests allow players to pledge allegiance to a single company, moving them from recruit status into the official company ranks.

| Grand Company | Enlistment quest | Client | Location | Basic requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maelstrom | Till Sea Swallows All | First Storm Lieutenant Orn Guincum | Maelstrom Command | Disciple of War or Magic level 25 or above; complete Ceruleum Shock |
| Order of the Twin Adder | Serenity, Purity, Sanctity | First Serpent Lieutenant Syro Fulke | The Adders' Nest | Disciple of War or Magic level 25 or above; complete Appetite for Destruction |
| Immortal Flames | By Fire Reborn | First Flame Lieutenant Hollin Aubrey | The Hall of Flames | Disciple of War or Magic level 25 or above; complete Know Your Enemy |

The patch notes also list the following questline-specific prerequisites for unlocking each enlistment quest:

| Enlistment quest | Maelstrom progress | Twin Adder progress | Immortal Flames progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Till Sea Swallows All | Storm Seals; Engineering Victory; An Officer and a Wise Man; Ceruleum Shock | Serpent Seals; Their Finest Hour; The Mail Must Get Through | Flame Seals; Burning Man; Arms Race |
| Serenity, Purity, Sanctity | Storm Seals; Engineering Victory; An Officer and a Wise Man | Serpent Seals; Their Finest Hour; The Mail Must Get Through; Appetite for Destruction | Flame Seals; Burning Man; Arms Race |
| By Fire Reborn | Storm Seals; Engineering Victory; An Officer and a Wise Man | Serpent Seals; Their Finest Hour; The Mail Must Get Through | Flame Seals; Burning Man; Arms Race; Know Your Enemy |
