# Faction guildleve reward audit

This audit covers all **53** known faction guildleves in the 1.x catalog: 20 Brotherhood of the Broken Blade, 19 Azeyma's Shields, and 14 Horn and Hand. It separates surviving retail evidence from the server's reconstructed values so a guess is never presented as retail fact.

## How to read the numbers

- **Current 1★ gil** is the server's guaranteed full-completion total: base levequest gil + completion gil + faction bonus gil. It excludes the optional treasure chest. A range means the current fallback formula changes with clear time.
- **Current EXP** is the current 1★ configured payout from the slowest eligible clear through an immediate clear. The server's `0.8` EXP scale is included.
- Almost every current gil override is explicitly marked **guessed/inferred** in the seed SQL. Retail-observed values are therefore shown separately and should win when implementing exact payouts.
- **GE** means the archived Gamer Escape page preserved in the local Wayback workbook. **eLeMeN** means the preserved Japanese faction-leve guide transcribed in this repository.
- `Moved from direct reward` identifies items that previously occupied the offer/completion item slots and now roll from the red chest. `GUESS moved` means the old mapping lacks surviving leve-specific proof.

## Shared chest behavior currently implemented

All **53** catalog faction leves now wait until every objective is complete and then spawn exactly one guaranteed red `GuildleveBonusTreasureBox` (`1200161`, `b923/e003`). This is explicit retail evidence for 27 leves; applying the same end-chest rule to the remaining 26 is deliberate reconstruction policy. Ordinary regional guildleves retain their separate 25–40% chest gate and blue/plated/gold reward tiers.

Every faction red chest contains gil and exactly one item. Forty-one leves have weighted, per-leve outcome tables; the other 12 use the shared level-band pool. A table entry with item ID zero deliberately selects that shared pool as a consolation outcome. These pools are shared only among faction leves and do not change ordinary regional-guildleve bonus chests. Custom additions are labeled separately from surviving retail evidence.

All faction item rewards have been removed from the direct offer/completion slots and merged into these red-chest tables. Former direct items generally keep a 10% individual chance. The five level-50 leves with existing runestone pools now use a custom 65% total runestone chance, divided equally among the existing stones, with 35% for their existing consolation rewards. Other level-50 tables and all level-20/30/40 rewards are unchanged. Unsupported stone guesses were discarded or corrected from the equipment dossier. The expanded tables also include the previously truncated Gamer Escape outcomes for Palemoon Parazuzu, Collecting Sea Shells, and Something in the Air.

- Level 20–30 shared pool: Cotton Coif, Cotton Sugarloaf Hat, Velveteen Bandana, Dodoskin Subligar, Silver Ingot, Undyed Cotton Cloth, Boar Leather, Strength Materia II, Vitality Materia II, Dexterity Materia II, Intelligence Materia II, Mind Materia II, Piety Materia II. Adding Dodoskin Subligar here is custom policy and makes it available across all three faction organizations.
- Level 40–50 shared pool: Woolen Hat, Woolen Coif, Woolen Beret, Mythril Ingot, Undyed Woolen Cloth, Raptor Leather, Strength Materia III, Vitality Materia III, Dexterity Materia III, Intelligence Materia III, Mind Materia III, Piety Materia III.
- Each armor/material entry has weight 25 and each materia entry has weight 1. The Level 20–30 faction pool has about **3.31% total materia chance** across six tier-II types (about **0.55% per type**) after adding Dodoskin Subligar. Level 40–50 retains about **3.85% total tier-III materia chance** (about **0.64% per type**).
- Red-chest gil is level 20 = 666; level 30 = 1,000; level 40 = 1,333; level 50 = 1,666. Exceptions: Operation: Supplication Denied uses 3,100 and Wanted: Rorogun the Tailtamer uses 4,499.
- Exact retail drop rates did not survive. Every moved direct item currently uses a provisional 10% chance; notable preexisting or custom pools are:
  - Operation: Supplication Denied: 10% each Blackened Scale Mail, Onion Doublet, and Onion Helm; 70% shared pool. The Onion equipment is a custom addition.
  - Operation: Bloody Side Up: 10% each Onion Gaskins, Onion Doublet, Tarnished Hoplon, and Hermes' Shoes; 60% shared pool. These are custom additions.
  - Operation: Reaving Home: 10% each Hermes' Shoes, Garlond Goggles, Onion Helm, Tarnished Hoplon, and Onion Pattens; 50% shared pool. These are custom additions.

  - Operation: Scar and Defeather: 10% Vintage Scale Mail; 10% Vintage Shepherd's Belt; 80% shared pool. These are user-supplied additions.
  - Operation: Frame Work: 10% Vintage Bill; 10% Vintage Round Shield; 80% shared pool. These are user-supplied additions.
  - Operation: Bloody Scales: 65% total for an equally selected Byregot, Thaliak, or Nophica Runestone (~21.67% each); 35% shared pool. The stone identities are an archival mission-chain association, while their placement on this particular terminal leve and the increased chance are custom reconstruction policy.
  - Operation: Under Siege: shared pool only. Its unsupported inherited Azeyma/Halone guess was removed.
  - Operation: Wolfsbane: 16.25% each Llymlaen, Halone, Azeyma, and Nymeia Runestone (65% total); 35% shared pool. The four-stone grouping is archival; placing it on this exact terminal leve and the increased chance are custom reconstruction policy.
  - Still Wanted: B'khenna the Phoenixfire: 10% each Vintage Shepherd's Belt, Vintage Seneschal Coatee, and Vintage Cudgel; 70% shared pool. These are user-supplied additions.
  - Wanted: Coiled Adder: 10% Vintage Guisarme; 90% shared pool. This is a user-supplied addition.

  - While They're Young: 10% Vintage Haubergeon; 10% Vintage Robe; 80% shared pool. These are user-supplied additions.
  - Operation: Pulling Fangs: 65% Allagan Runestone - Menphina; 35% split evenly among six elemental Crystal x4 outcomes (~5.83% each).
  - Operation: Shuteye: 16.25% each for the Azeyma, Halone, Llymlaen, and Nymeia runestones (65% total); 35% shared pool.
  - Operation: Crosseye: 10% Coliseum Subligar; 90% shared pool.
  - Wanted: Mammoth and Master: 16.25% each for the Althyk, Nald'thal, Oschon, and Rhalgr runestones (65% total); 35% shared pool.

  - Wanted: Vengeance: 10% Coliseum Shawl; 40% Dodoskin Subligar; 40% Boarskin Harness; 10% shared pool.
  - Intel Outside: 10% Coliseum Loincloth; 90% shared pool.

The archived eLeMeN objectives explicitly say **completion spawns a treasure chest for 27 faction leves**: 13 Broken Blade, 13 Azeyma's Shields, and Intel Outside. The broader 53-leve guarantee is intentionally identified as reconstructed behavior rather than silently relabeled as retail fact.

The client shows only two main reward slots. Base gil occupies the first slot, leaving one visible direct-item slot; faction credits and the bonus-gil lines are separate. Faction item slots are now intentionally empty, and the guaranteed red chest owns the complete multi-option item roll instead.

## Findings that need implementation decisions

1. **All 53 faction leves now end with one red chest.** Twenty-seven have explicit eLeMeN completion-chest wording; extending the guarantee to the other 26 is reconstruction policy requested for this server.
2. **Thirty-eight weighted per-leve pools are implemented.** Evidence-backed, user-supplied, and inherited guessed item identities are labeled separately from provisional probability weights.
3. **All faction item rewards now come from red chests.** The 53 faction direct offer/completion item slots are intentionally empty; gil, EXP, and faction credits remain on their existing channels.
4. **The old two-slot truncation is resolved:** Palemoon Parazuzu now includes Onion Pattens and Aldgoat Steak x2, Collecting Sea Shells includes Hempen Bowstring, and Something in the Air includes Horn Glue.
5. **All 12 deity runestones are represented in red-chest tables.** The corresponding Templar, Buccaneer, and Harlequin equipment is intentionally excluded because those finished pieces came from Rowena barter, not directly from the faction chest. The four Coliseum pieces remain direct random chest items.
6. **Unverified inherited mappings remain, but only as chest candidates:** Operation: Sylph Stalkings; Operation: Warm Welcome; Wanted: Xha Viqqoh the Nibbler; and Unlike a Rolling Stone. Bloody Scales and Wolfsbane are now separately labeled as archival chain/grouping associations; Under Siege has no guessed unique item.
7. **Fifteen red chests currently use only the shared level-band pool.** This guarantees useful loot without inventing a unique retail association and leaves a clean data surface for adding leve-specific drops.
8. **Gil is mostly reconstructed:** the current seed values should not be treated as retail-exact. Where the workbook has observed gil, the observed figures are included row-by-row; where it does not, the exact retail amount remains unknown.
