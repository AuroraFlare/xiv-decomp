# Garuda Hard: named body-animation evidence, 2026-09-07

Later evidence below adds two named Monk-recording Mistral Shrieks and a
Plumage rise/release. The combined bundle now retains 46 full frames. The original
three-action analysis is preserved; native joins are evaluated separately in
[the pose comparison](garuda-pose-comparison-2026-09-07.md).

## Useful distinction

The supplied original-FFXIV-1.x recording gives three independently **named**
body-action sequences suitable for comparison with native motion data:

| Named action | Most useful visual interval | Positive body observation | Confidence/limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wicked Wheel | 326.000–326.625 | Rapid whole-body turn with wings sweeping horizontally around Garuda; several successive facings are visible | Strongest rotational evidence; no exact turn count, handedness or native rotation axis assigned |
| Downburst | 390.250–390.750; corroborating 477.625–478.125 | Rears/rises, conspicuously extends a leg, then dips through a downward sweeping turn | Strong rise/rear/leg-and-dip sequence; **not** proof of zero yaw or a purely vertical flap |
| Slipstream | 415.500–416.500 | Holds a forward-facing crouched/curled-wing posture, opens the wings, then sweeps them down/forward and recovers | Distinct body sequence; overlapping spells prevent attribution of every visible flash or gust |

**No WSS or native motion number is assigned in this report.** The task here is
to provide positive name-to-body observations for the separate native-motion
comparison, not to guess the resource mapping from ability names or VFX colors.

## Source and method

All decisive frames come from the [user-supplied recording, `rzVsuAo30hs`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rzVsuAo30hs),
using the already cached 1280x720 video at 23.976024 fps. Existing Monk/WHM samples
were also checked, but did not provide a stronger named/body pair in this bounded
pass. There were no downloads, production edits or client-preview operations.

Newly appended ready lines were located separately from persistent old lines.
The subsequent body sequence was sampled at 0.125–0.25-second spacing and decisive
frames were viewed individually at full resolution. This is sampled video
inspection, not a claim of unfiltered combat-log or packet access. Timestamps
are video-presentation seconds, not battle elapsed time or exact cast durations.

The [artifact README](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/README.md)
contains reproduction commands and source provenance. Thirty unchanged full
frames retain the action-name log, target identity, body and surrounding effects.
Search grids/bulk frames are ignored rather than promoted to primary evidence.

A new `Garuda readies Wicked Wheel.` line appears after the first post-Aerial

and is fully readable in the
[323.625 full frame](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/wicked-wheel/frame-323.625s.jpg).
There is no intervening newly named Garuda action before the following motion;
the next newly appended Slipstream line is not present until roughly 329.5.

The body is partly out of frame initially, but returns for the useful execution:

- 324.875–325.625: Garuda holds the broad four-wing presentation in front of the
  tank. Those wings are her model, not a player effect.
- [326.250](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/wicked-wheel/frame-326.250s.jpg)
  and [326.500](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/wicked-wheel/frame-326.500s.jpg):
  the body, head, feathers and wing silhouette turn together, showing successive
  facings while the nearby players/terrain remain comparatively stable. This

- 326.125–326.625: circular ribbons surround the turning body. They corroborate
  the sweep, but the body motion itself is the decisive evidence.
- By 326.750–327.250 the body has risen toward the top of the frame again and
  player hit/heal effects overlap the aftermath.

The safe comparison label is **rapid whole-body horizontal turning sweep**.
Video alone does not identify a skeletal root-axis sign or prove that the model
completed exactly one revolution. The earlier jump/Aerial Blast text persists
in the pane and must not be used to relabel this newly named Wheel sequence.

## Downburst: rear/rise, extended leg, downward sweeping dip

### Clearer central-camera instance

At 388.00 the pane still contains old **Suparna** Downburst lines but no newly
appended **Garuda** Downburst. By the
[389.500 frame](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/downburst/frame-389.500s.jpg),
`Garuda readies Downburst.` is newly appended. This actor-name distinction is
important: the observed model is the selected Garuda after the sister sequence,
not a Suparna action inferred from a persistent line.

- 390.000: body still centered in the preparation pose.
- [390.250](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/downburst/frame-390.250s.jpg):
  she rears and rises, exposing the legs below/forward of the torso.
- [390.500](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/downburst/frame-390.500s.jpg):
  the model is higher and a leg is extended conspicuously toward screen-right;
  this is a recognizable skeletal pose rather than a bright AoE flash.
- [390.750](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/downburst/frame-390.750s.jpg):
  she is substantially lower, dipping through a sweeping turn, with wings and
  torso changing attitude; recovery continues into 391.000.

This can be called a **rearing/leg-extension/downward-sweep sequence**. It must
not be simplified to “no spin”: turning is also visible, and its exact rotation
axis or amount is not recoverable confidently from these camera frames.

The orange shell and `HP Boost` labels during 389.5–390.5 overlap the player's
Battle Voice activity. The green ground effect around 391 seconds overlaps
Ballad of Magi. They are not independently established Garuda VFX, and their
shape/color must not be used to assign Downburst's resource.

## Slipstream: held preparation, front-facing wing-driven release

A new `Garuda readies Slipstream.` line is absent at 411.500 and fully readable
in the [412.000 frame](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/slipstream/frame-412.000s.jpg).
No new differently named Garuda action intervenes before the sequence below.

- 412–415.5: a prolonged, forward-facing crouched/curled-wing preparation is
  visible amid player attacks. Orange streaks alone do not identify the skill.
- [415.875](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/slipstream/frame-415.875s.jpg):
  Garuda opens the wings broadly while keeping the torso generally toward the
  same frontal direction.
- [416.125](../outputs/garuda-named-action-video-20260907/slipstream/frame-416.125s.jpg):
  the wings sweep down/forward; the body remains identifiable rather than
  turning through the strongly changing facings seen in the Wheel example.
- 416.5 onward: recovery; several player effects overlap the scene.

The safe motion-comparison label is **held frontal preparation followed by a
wing-driven forward/downward release**. This sequence does not show a comparable
rapid full-body yaw sweep. It does not, however, quantify a hit cone, launch
vector or client root motion.

A second newly named Slipstream at 465–466 seconds was checked through 469.5.
It has a similar held preparation followed by movement near 468–469 seconds, but
the camera pans and bright lightning/orange/white player effects obscure the
body. It is not used as the main resource-disambiguation example.

## Damage/status evidence and what remains unproven

No decisive selected frame contains a newly appended **named Garuda skill →
recipient → numeric damage/status** result for these three actions. The pane
primarily supplies ready lines. Floating values and party HP changes overlap
player attacks, cures, buffs, autoattacks and several actors, so **no new damage
or status calibration is extracted here**. In particular, a floating number
below Garuda is not automatically her damage to the tank.

The positive deliverable is a set of named body sequences. Native WSS identity,
skeletal-track axis/sign, exact cast/effect timing, damage radius, damage amount,
status application and knockback still require their separate evidence. These
frames are useful to compare with decoded poses but do not replace that join.
