> * [dev1132] Several adjustments have been made to overall experience point rewards and the current party bonus algorithm.The following are used to calculate experience point rewards:
> 1. Level-based EXP
>
>        The general rule for calculating experience point awards is to divide the amount of experience needed to level up by the number of encounters the current level* is intended to last. The amount of EXP yielded increases proportional to character level.
>
> * The level used in this calculation is either the player's or the enemy's, whichever is lower.
> 2. Difference Between Player and Enemy Levels
>
>        Higher level enemies will yield more EXP, while lower level enemies will yield less. This revision will not greatly affect the amount of EXP obtainable. However, a cap will be imposed upon the bonus awarded to players for defeating higher level enemies.
> 3. Party Size
>
>        Each additional member in the party reduces the amount of experience points yielded. Although the reduction is greater than before, it will be offset by the bonuses granted by EXP chains and linking.
> 4. Enemy Type
>
>        As the variance in strength between enemies has been reduced (see below), enemy type will not influence EXP yield.
>
>        * The exception to the above is those enemies deemed extremely weak or strong.
> 5. Link Bonus (see below)
>
>        Experience point bonuses are now rewarded for defeating enemies which appear together with the initial target.
> 6. EXP Chain (see below)
>
>        Defeating enemies in quick succession will now earn players experience point bonuses.
> 7. Guardian's Aspect
>
>       Invoking Guardian's Aspect when activating a levequest will grant players an experience point bonus upon quest completion.
> 8. Gear
>
>        There are several pieces of low-level gear which grant their wearers experience point bonuses.
> * [dev1144] Most damage algorithms have been renewed in conjunction with the abolition of physical levels and the automation of character attributes.These changes serve to streamline the influence of damage algorithms upon battle attribute calculations.
> * [dev1027] Enemy attributes have been adjusted.Certain varieties of enemies who were disproportionately strong for their level have been adjusted in order to make an enemy's level a more accurate indication of it's strength. Based on this revision, enemy characteristics such as strong attack or defense will be kept to within the bounds of level.
> * [dev1144] The damage bonus uniformly granted to attacks dealt from the back and side of an enemy has been abolished.Only certain monsters will exhibit vulnerability to attacks from specific directions and will take additional damage in such instances. This change does not affect the accuracy bonus currently in place for attacks dealt under the same conditions.

> * [dev1026] The system of claiming enemies has been overhauled.Until now, only players who claimed a target would receive experience points, loot, and fulfill quest objectives for defeating it. > **Known Issues**
> * An issue wherein equipping gear that affects maximum HP while resting in passive mode resets HP and MP recovery.
> * An issue where shield actions may not be accompanied by their animation.
> * An issue wherein certain actions have been renamed for 1.19, but this change is not reflected in help text.
> * An issue wherein the help text of certain traits that influence attributes (such as Muscle Memory) still mentions point allocation despite it being abolished as of patch 1.19.
>
> * Such traits have been changed to instead affect attributes that increase automatically through leveling.
> * An issue has been reported wherein the tutorial will freeze temporarily for characters created prior to patch 1.19 who do not have a weaponskill equipped.
>
> The tutorial will automatically resume after approximately seven minutes have elapsed. We apologize for any inconvenience caused.
> * An issue wherein information on equippable class is showing incorrectly for the following gear:Angler's Hat / Angler's Hat (Blue)
>
> * Both items are functioning as intended.
> * An issue wherein the following gear grant unintended bonus effects:Mythril Haubergeon / Cobalt Haubergeon / Sheepskin Mitts / Leather Mitts
> * A bug wherein the color of an enemy’s display name does not update properly under certain circumstances, such as changes to party composition during combat, or players being KO’d. The bug applies to the color of the display name only, and in no way affects the claim over the enemy, nor the rights to any experience points or loot resulting from its defeat.
> * An issue wherein untradable items that are stackable do not stack as intended when received from another player via the Loot List.
> * A bug wherein the Gear Change status is not incurred by changing gear during combat.
> * A bug wherein players who logged out as a Disciple of the Hand class prior to patch 1.19 and have since not changed to a class of another Discipline are unable to affix materia to items.
>
