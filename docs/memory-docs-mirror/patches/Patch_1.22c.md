# Patch 1.22c


**Source:** https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/48097-patch1.22c-Patch-1.22c-Notes%7C

> **Event**
> * **[dev1445] The concluding part of the quest “A Relic Reborn” has been added.**
>
> * This repeatable quest is necessary to obtain special job-specific weaponry, and will require a great deal of time and dedication to complete.
>
>
>
> Based on the above addition, the following adjustments have been made:
> * New treasure chests have been added to Dzemael Darkhold.
> * Monsters in certain behests now drop new items.
>
> * These items can be obtained regardless of quest acceptance.
> * New rewards have been added for high-level caravan escort missions.
> * New faction leves have been added.
> * A more difficult battle against Ifrit, fought in the Bowl of Embers (Extreme), has been added.
>
> * In order to gain access to the Bowl of Embers (Extreme), you must have accepted the quest “A Relic Reborn” and fulfilled certain conditions. Furthermore, only parties consisting of eight Disciples of War or Magic of level 50 or higher will be permitted to enter.

> **≪Getting Promoted≫**
>
> Adventurers must fulfill the following requirements if they wish to rise from Chief Sergeant to Second Lieutenant:
> 1. Complete one of the following Grand Company quests:
>
>
>
>
>
> (Table Content)
> Grand Company | Quest Title
> Maelstrom | Patrol, Interrupted
> Order of the Twin Adder | Cure for the Common Pox
> Immortal Flames | Mess with the Goat, Get the Horns
> 2. Complete one of the three “United We Stand” quests.
> 3. Pay 25,000 company seals.
>
> * Depending on your Grand Company, you will receive either the Aquamarine Cross, the Emerald Bough, or the Platinum Scales in commemoration of your promotion.
>
>
>
>
