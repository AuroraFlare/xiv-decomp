# Dzemael Darkhold 1.x: online maps and mob-location source search

Research date: 2026-09-15. Scope: original Final Fantasy XIV, especially 2011–2012; Japanese, English-language NA/UK/international, Taiwanese, French and German sources.

## Result

**Four surviving map sets were verified, plus encounter diagrams. No verified source plots every individual original-version mob spawn across the entire dungeon.** The sets overlap: the Taiwanese route maps visibly reuse the Japanese guide's lettering and artwork, with additional annotations. They are not four independent surveys.

The most useful combination is Elemen's detailed guide and encounter diagrams, Archylte's dated Japanese route maps and room notes, the Taiwanese tactical drawings, and the preserved English Gamer Escape maps/roster. These provide room-level and selected formation evidence. They do not establish a complete coordinate database or exact ordinary-mob homes.

This search adds source material only. It does not revise the existing 151 placements, formations, combat mechanics, SQL, or client-validation status.

It omits some encounter adds, so it is not a complete actor census.

## 6. Attractive results that belong to ARR

- [FFXIVInfo's Dzemael page](https://www.ffxivinfo.com/dungeons/dzemael-darkhold) identifies patch 2.0. It is not a source for the original encounter layout.
- [Filica's Japanese Lodestone guide](https://jp.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/5837967/blog/1055502/) has detailed maps but explicitly targets version 2.2. Its enemy dots must not be imported into 1.x.
- [Crystal Universe's German guide](https://crystaluniverse.de/guides/final-fantasy/ffxiv-dungeon-guide-feste-dzemael/) belongs to ARR and describes subsequent layout changes.
- [Current Gamer Escape maps](https://ffxiv.gamerescape.com/wiki/Dzemael_Darkhold/Maps) are distinct from the orphan legacy files above.
- The [Fandom page labelled version 1.0](https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Dzemael_Darkhold_(version_1.0)) mixes legacy material with an ARR-style boss listing in indexed content. The label alone is insufficient validation.

## 7. Follow-up: additional mob and location evidence

The second search recovered specific pack counts, historical grid references and six additional photographs. It did not recover a fifth map set or a complete individual-spawn plot.

### Japanese launch-day report: ordinary enemy groups

[Guard's post #6, July 22, 2011](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/17111?p=245460&viewfull=1#post245460) describes a firsthand run and divides the route at the inter-map transporter.

| Region | Enemy description | Reported group size |
|---|---|---:|
| Before transporter | Hippogryphs | Usually 2 |
| Before transporter | Humanoid enemies | Usually 2; singles also observed |
| Before transporter | Anglerfish / orobon | 4 |
| Before transporter | Hedgemoles | Approximately 6 |
| Before transporter | Frogs; skeletons | Singles |
| After transporter | New ghost / “bogey” type | 3 |

The author places hippogryphs, orobon, frogs and humanoids earlier, with hedgemoles and skeletons farther in. These are regional observations, not counts for every pack. “Bogey” is a generic description, not a verified native actor identity.

[Post #7](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/17111?p=246200&viewfull=1#post246200) mentions extra ghosts near the ogre room's northwest entrance and southwest. Its claimed third formation remains uncorroborated. [Post #9](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/17111?p=247250&viewfull=1#post247250) associates later ghosts/frogs, a terminal-triggered skeleton group and southern-room imperials. Preserve these as launch-era testimony.

### English cross-check for the final room

[Kai Crystallis's Notorious Monster Guide](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/31475-Kai-Crystallis-Presents-Notorious-Monster-Guide), first posted November 27, 2011 and revised in December, also records Batraal at **(8,4)**. ## Evidence preserved and practical conclusion

New unmodified image bytes, source links, dimensions, dates, SHA-256 hashes and interpretation notes are recorded in `Data/raidroutes/evidence/dzemael-map-search-20260915/manifest.json`. Ten assets were preserved: six route/base-map images, two tactical images, one reward diagram and one terminal photograph. Each was decoded and visually checked. Core Elemen maps/overlays/encounter diagrams remain in `Data/raidroutes/evidence/dzemael-20260914/`.

The follow-up adds **six images and three decoded page captures** under `Data/raidroutes/evidence/dzemael-map-search-20260915/followup/`, with a separate manifest. All nine file hashes were checked. nWiki's browser verification is recorded separately because a durable page export was unavailable. The combined search preservation now contains **16 image assets**, in addition to the pre-existing Elemen collection.

The online evidence can support a **composite, explicitly sourced room/encounter map**. It does not justify calling such a reconstruction a recovered map of every mob. Ordinary mobs between the annotated landmarks, precise counts/homes, facing, patrols and elevations remain incompletely documented. No live-client acceptance or ground validation follows from this research.
